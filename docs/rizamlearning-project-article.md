# AIR-003 — Statistics for AI

How do we know if an observed model improvement is meaningful? This educational project implements statistical methods and simulates known statistical conditions. The eight experiments evaluate sampling, confidence interval coverage, test rejection rates, resampling, power and false discoveries. At n=5 with unknown population SD, t interval coverage was 95.09% compared to 87.78% for the plug-in normal interval. In families of 20 independent null tests, at least one false rejection occurred in 64.65% of unadjusted families and around 5% after correction. These are simulation estimates, not results on real AI systems. BH controls FDR under its assumptions, not generally FWER. See the research report for all 27 configurations and limitations. The public repository includes source, eight teaching notebooks and reproduction commands. Release status must be independently updated after CI and audit.
